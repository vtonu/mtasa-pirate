# PIRATES IN MEN'S PANTS

## OVERVIEW

Pirates in Men's Pants is a casino and hotel located on The Strip in Las Venturas, San Andreas. The landmark is instantly recognizable by its massive pirate ship, making it one of the most memorable locations in the city.

For this custom server, Pirates in Men's Pants serves as the central mission hub and primary point of interest. The pirate ship acts as the heart of gameplay, where players gather, begin missions, earn rewards, and interact with server content.

## LOCATION

- City: Las Venturas
- District: The Strip
- State: San Andreas
- Nearby Landmarks:
  - The High Roller
  - The Visage

## SERVER PURPOSE

The pirate ship functions as the main activity area for the server.

Players can:

- Start missions
- Collect rewards
- Participate in special events
- Access mission objectives
- Meet other players
- Earn money and progression rewards

The location was chosen because of its unique visual identity and strong connection to treasure hunting, piracy, exploration, and adventure.

## DESIGN THEME

The server embraces a pirate inspired atmosphere built around:

- Treasure hunting
- Hidden rewards
- Risk versus reward gameplay
- Special collectibles
- Mission progression
- Exploration

The pirate ship acts as a symbol of adventure and serves as the centerpiece of the entire experience.
